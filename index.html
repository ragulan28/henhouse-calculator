Frequently Asked Questions

How is the live chicken selling price calculated?

The break-even price per kilogram is calculated by dividing total cost (purchase cost + expenses per bird) by the live weight. The final selling price is then calculated using your desired profit margin percentage: Selling Price = Break-Even Price / (1 - Margin/100).

What is the standard dressing yield for broiler chicken in Sri Lanka?

In Sri Lanka, standard commercial broiler dressing yield typically ranges from 68% to 74% (average 71%), depending on bird age, feed quality, and dressed preparation (skin-on, whole cleaned bird).

Why is margin calculated on selling price instead of cost markup?

Commercial poultry traders and retail butcher shops use gross profit margin (Profit / Selling Price), ensuring your actual return percentage matches total sales revenue rather than just marking up initial purchase cost.
